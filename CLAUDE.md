## Thesis

BS Meter exists to answer one question: **is this game worth your time?** Every scored measure has to tie back to that, meaning it either wastes the player's time (the BS side) or earns it (the Juice side). Things that are not about time (pricing, business model, microtransactions) do not get their own scored measure; price enters through the verdict, not the ratings.
